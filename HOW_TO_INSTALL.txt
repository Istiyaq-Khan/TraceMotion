================================================================================
  TRACEMOTION STUDIO: SCREEN RECORDING & MOTION ENHANCEMENT SUITE
  1:1 Recordly Parity Engine (Zero-Electron C++ OBS Plugin + After Effects Rig)
================================================================================

TraceMotion captures high-fidelity 60Hz/120Hz system cursor telemetry, click events,
and real-time cursor shape classifications in OBS Studio via a native C++20 plugin,
then reconstructs studio-grade pan/zoom motion, spring-physics cursor tracking,
multi-state cursor rendering, typing speed-up, and window drop-shadow framing in
Adobe After Effects.

2. INSTALLING THE OBS STUDIO PLUGIN (C++ TELEMETRY RECORDER)
================================================================================
The compiled native 64-bit plugin is located at:
  obs-telemetry-recorder\build\tracemotion-obs.dll

Automated One-Click Install:
  1. In File Explorer, go to the repository root: D:\all my code\TraceMotion\
  2. Double-click "install_obs_plugin.bat".
  3. Click "Yes" on the Windows UAC elevation prompt.
  4. The installer automatically updates your OBS plugin folders:
     - User AppData: %APPDATA%\obs-studio\plugins\tracemotion-obs\
     - Global ProgramData: C:\ProgramData\obs-studio\plugins\tracemotion-obs\
     - Program Files: C:\Program Files\obs-studio\obs-plugins\64bit\

Zero-Configuration Background Operation:
  TraceMotion operates as an automatic background recorder. Whenever you click
  "Start Recording" in OBS Studio, TraceMotion hooks into the recording lifecycle
  and begins logging cursor telemetry, click events, and cursor shapes. When you
  stop recording, it automatically writes a matching ".cursor.json" companion file
  alongside your video in the exact same directory.

3. INSTALLING THE AFTER EFFECTS SCRIPTUI PANEL & IMPORTER
================================================================================
The After Effects script and its assets are located in:
  after-effects-importer\

Step 1: Locate your After Effects "ScriptUI Panels" directory:
  Typical path:
    D:\apps\Adobe After Effects 2020\Support Files\Scripts\ScriptUI Panels\
    (or C:\Program Files\Adobe\Adobe After Effects <Version>\Support Files\Scripts\ScriptUI Panels\)

Step 2: Copy the script and assets folder:
  Copy:
    after-effects-importer\TraceMotionImporter.jsx
  And the folder:
    after-effects-importer\assets\   (containing 'cursors' and 'wallpapers')
  Into your ScriptUI Panels folder:
    Support Files\Scripts\ScriptUI Panels\
      TraceMotionImporter.jsx
      assets\
        cursors\
          manifest.json
          macos\
          windows11\
          tahoe\
          custom\
        wallpapers\
          manifest.json
          (39 wallpaper files)

Step 3: Enable Scripting Permissions in After Effects:
  - Open Adobe After Effects.
  - Go to: "Edit" -> "Preferences" -> "Scripting & Expressions..."
    (In older versions: "Edit" -> "Preferences" -> "General").
  - Check the box: "Allow Scripts to Write Files and Access Network".
  - Click OK.

Step 4: Launch the Studio Panel:
  - In After Effects, open the top menu: "Window" -> "TraceMotionImporter.jsx".
  - The dark-studio panel docks anywhere in your workspace.
  - Features the modern 3-tab layout (Setup, Cursor, Frame) matching the HTML prototype,
    complete with interactive live composition preview, zero emojis, and zero clipping.

4. RECORDING WORKFLOW (OBS STUDIO)
================================================================================
1. Launch OBS Studio. Ensure your screen or window capture source is configured.
2. Start Recording in OBS (click "Start Recording" or your assigned hotkey).
   - TraceMotion starts automatically.
3. Perform your workflow, software demo, or tutorial normally:
   - Move cursor between buttons, links, text fields, and window edges.
   - Click, select text, and type normally.
4. Stop Recording.
5. In your recording folder (e.g. C:\Users\<Username>\Videos\), two synchronized
   files are generated:
   - Video: 2026-11-19 01-56-53.mp4
   - Telemetry: 2026-11-19 01-56-53.mp4.cursor.json

5. IMPORTING & MOTION RIG GENERATION (AFTER EFFECTS)
================================================================================
1. Open the TraceMotion panel in After Effects ("Window" -> "TraceMotionImporter.jsx").
2. Tab 1: Setup
   - Click "Browse..." next to Capture Source and pick your recorded MP4.
   - The companion telemetry JSON is auto-detected and paired with a green status badge.
   - Camera Zoom Mode: Auto Zoom, Follow, or Static.
   - Zoom Depth: Set zoom multiplier (default: 1.80x).
   - Motion Feel: Select "Natural", "Snappy", "Silky", or "Raw".
   - Fine-Tune Physics Accordion: Toggle Auto Cursor States, Velocity Tilt Sway,
     Click Bounce, Motion Blur, and Typing Speed-Up.
3. Tab 2: Cursor
   - Cursor Pack: Choose macOS Tahoe, macOS Classic, Windows 11, or Figma Minimal.
   - Cursor Size: 50% to 200% (default: 100%).
   - Click Ripple: Choose Cobalt, White, Emerald, Amber, or Off.
4. Tab 3: Frame
   - Backdrop: Select visual tiles (Tahoe, Midnight, Azure, Cherry, Studio, None)
     or pick from the full 39 Recordly wallpaper dropdown.
   - Window Framing Sliders: Corner Radius (default: 20px), Window Margin (12%),
     Drop Shadow Intensity (65%).
5. Click "Generate Motion Comp" (bottom action bar).

6. AFTER EFFECTS RIG ARCHITECTURE & KEY FEATURES
================================================================================
Layer Hierarchy:
----------------
1. TraceMotion_Click_Ripples (Two-Tier Shockwave Shape Layer)
   - Tier 1: Soft inner pop disc (0 to 14px radius, 120ms fade).
   - Tier 2: Outer shockwave ring (4 to 48px radius, 450ms cubic ease-out,
     stroke width tapers 3.2px -> 0.6px).
   - Centered directly at calibrated click hotspots with zero ExtendScript reference errors.
2. Cursor State Layers (parented to TraceMotion_Cursor_Master)
   - 10 multi-states: arrow, pointer, text, crosshair, open-hand, closed-hand,
     resize-ew, resize-ns, not-allowed, wait.
   - Calibrated anchor point on each state layer.
   - Clean Hold keyframes on Opacity.
3. TraceMotion_Cursor_Master (Null Layer parented to Camera Rig)
   - Damped harmonic oscillator spring position.
   - Dynamic velocity-based tilt sway rotation.
   - Click bounce compression (84% depression).
   - Drag & Text Selection Lock: 1:1 near-zero latency tracking during text
     highlighting so cursor stays locked to the on-screen highlight.
4. TraceMotion_Camera_Controller (Null Guide Layer)
   - Live expression sliders: Zoom Scale, Canvas Margin, Corner Radius,
     Shadow Intensity, Cursor Sway, Camera Focus point.
5. TraceMotion_Camera_Rig (3D Master Null Layer)
   - Straight-Line Spatial Zoom: Linear spatial tangents with cubic temporal ease
     completely eliminate the "boomerang" wobble / curving upward artifact.
   - Recordly spring overshoot guard eliminates counter-oscillation on reversals.
6. TraceMotion_Window_Matte (Shape Layer, Alpha Track Matte for Video)
   - Re-anchored to [0, 0] preventing 1/4 screen clipping bugs.
7. Clean_Screen_Capture (Video Footage parented to Rig)
   - Typing Zoom & 2.0x Time Remapping: Automatically speeds up prolonged typing
     segments by 2.0x via AE Time Remapping keyframes while zooming into the input field.
8. TraceMotion_Window_Shadow (Shape Layer with 3 Drop Shadows)
   - Ambient, Body, and Contact drop shadows unclipped by track matte.
9. TraceMotion_Background (Wallpaper Footage or Gradient)
   - Cover-scaled to canvas.

8. TROUBLESHOOTING & FAQ
================================================================================
Q: I get "ReferenceError: Function Object.keys is undefined Line: 1673".
A: Resolved! Adobe After Effects (particularly AE 2020) runs on ECMAScript 3 (1999).
   TraceMotion now provides native ES3 polyfills and a safe `getObjectKeys()` helper,
   allowing the script to run seamlessly across AE CC 2020 through CC 2026+.

Q: The script opens two windows, or a floating window that cannot be docked.
A: Resolved! The script now detects native ScriptUI Panel hosts and passes the execution
   scope directly, populating After Effects' docked panel without creating or showing
   a secondary floating window.

Q: The panel stretches with large empty white spaces.
A: Resolved! Layout containers are now locked to top alignment with compact 6px margins,
   a 96px interactive preview canvas, and compact tab panels fitting any docked height.

Q: The camera wobbled or looped upward before diving into zoom targets.
A: Resolved! Replaced anchor-point animation with Recordly's 1:1 monotonic straight-line
   transform math from `zoomTransform.ts`. The camera rig origin stays fixed at [0, 0]
   and moves directly to the focus point with zero reverse motion or upward drift.

Q: The mouse cursor lagged behind text selection in recorded footage.
A: Resolved! Telemetry drag detection locks the cursor directly to raw coordinates with
   linear spatial interpolation during text highlighting, so the cursor leads the on-screen
   highlight 1:1 with zero latency.

Q: Are click ripples included?
A: Yes! With the ES3 crash resolved, every click produces a two-tier ripple: an inner
   pop disc (120ms fade) and an expanding outer shockwave ring (450ms, cubic ease-out)
   positioned at the calibrated cursor hotspot.
