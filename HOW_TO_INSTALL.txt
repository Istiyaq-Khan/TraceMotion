================================================================================
  TRACEMOTION: PROFESSIONAL SCREEN RECORDING & MOTION ENHANCEMENT SUITE
  1:1 Recordly Parity Engine (Zero-Electron C++ OBS Plugin + After Effects Rig)
================================================================================

TraceMotion captures high-fidelity 60Hz/120Hz system cursor telemetry, click events,
and real-time cursor shape classifications in OBS Studio via a native C++20 plugin,
then reconstructs studio-grade pan/zoom motion, spring-physics cursor tracking,
multi-state cursor rendering, and window drop-shadow framing in Adobe After Effects.

2. INSTALLING THE OBS STUDIO PLUGIN (C++ TELEMETRY RECORDER)
================================================================================
The compiled native 64-bit plugin is located at:
  obs-telemetry-recorder\build\tracemotion-obs.dll

Step 1: Close OBS Studio if it is currently running.

Step 2: Copy the plugin DLL:
  Copy:
    D:\all my code\TraceMotion\obs-telemetry-recorder\build\tracemotion-obs.dll
  To:
    C:\Program Files\obs-studio\obs-plugins\64bit\tracemotion-obs.dll

Step 3: Copy the plugin localization data:
  Copy the folder:
    D:\all my code\TraceMotion\obs-telemetry-recorder\data\
  To:
    C:\Program Files\obs-studio\data\obs-plugins\tracemotion-obs\

  (Create the "tracemotion-obs" folder inside "data\obs-plugins" if it does not exist.
   Ensure "en-US.ini" is located at:
   C:\Program Files\obs-studio\data\obs-plugins\tracemotion-obs\locale\en-US.ini)

Step 4: Launch OBS Studio.
  - In OBS Studio, open the top menu: "Tools" -> "TraceMotion Telemetry Recorder".
  - Check "Enable Telemetry Recording".
  - Set "Sampling Rate" to 60 Hz (standard) or 120 Hz (ultra-smooth).
  - Leave "Output Directory" empty to automatically save JSON files alongside your
    OBS video recordings, or browse to a custom folder.

3. INSTALLING THE AFTER EFFECTS SCRIPTUI PANEL & IMPORTER
================================================================================
The After Effects script and its assets are located in:
  after-effects-importer\

Step 1: Locate your After Effects "ScriptUI Panels" directory:
  Typical path:
    C:\Program Files\Adobe\Adobe After Effects <Version>\Support Files\Scripts\ScriptUI Panels\

Step 2: Copy the script and assets folder:
  Copy:
    after-effects-importer\TraceMotionImporter.jsx
  And the folder:
    after-effects-importer\assets\   (containing 'cursors' and 'wallpapers')
  Into:
    C:\Program Files\Adobe\Adobe After Effects <Version>\Support Files\Scripts\ScriptUI Panels\TraceMotion\

  Folder structure should look like:
    ScriptUI Panels\
      TraceMotion\
        TraceMotionImporter.jsx
        assets\
          cursors\
            manifest.json
            macos\
            windows11\
            tahoe\
            custom\
          wallpapers\
            manifest.json
            (39 wallpaper image and video files)

  NOTE: You can also keep the files directly in `D:\all my code\TraceMotion\after-effects-importer\`
  and run it via AE menu: "File" -> "Scripts" -> "Run Script File...".

Step 3: Enable Scripting Permissions in After Effects:
  - Open Adobe After Effects.
  - Go to: "Edit" -> "Preferences" -> "Scripting & Expressions..."
    (In older versions: "Edit" -> "Preferences" -> "General").
  - Check the box: "Allow Scripts to Write Files and Access Network".
  - Click OK.

Step 4: Dock the Panel:
  - In After Effects, open the top menu: "Window" -> "TraceMotionImporter.jsx".
  - Dock the TraceMotion panel anywhere in your After Effects workspace (e.g. alongside
    Effects & Presets or Composition preview).
  - The panel features a responsive dark-studio interface with smooth mouse-wheel
    scrolling and live SVG vector previews.

4. RECORDING WORKFLOW (OBS STUDIO)
================================================================================
1. Launch OBS Studio. Ensure your screen or window capture source is configured.
2. Start Recording (click "Start Recording" or your assigned hotkey).
   - TraceMotion will silently record high-precision cursor position, click events,
     press/release timestamps, and classified cursor shapes.
3. Perform your workflow, software demo, or tutorial normally.
4. Stop Recording.
5. In your recording folder, you will see two synchronized files:
   - Video: `2026-11-18 02-30-00.mp4` (or .mkv)
   - Telemetry: `2026-11-18 02-30-00.tracemotion.json`

5. IMPORTING & MOTION RIG GENERATION (AFTER EFFECTS)
================================================================================
1. Open the TraceMotion panel in After Effects ("Window" -> "TraceMotionImporter.jsx").
2. Source Files:
   - Click "Browse..." next to Video File and select your recorded video.
   - Click "Browse..." next to Telemetry JSON. If the JSON file has the same name and
     directory as the video, TraceMotion auto-detects it.
3. Canvas & Framing:
   - Select Canvas Preset:
     * 1080p (1920x1080 @ 60fps) - Standard YouTube / Web
     * 4K UHD (3840x2160 @ 60fps) - Ultra High Definition
     * 9:16 Vertical (1080x1920 @ 60fps) - TikTok / Shorts / Reels
     * Custom resolution & framerate
   - Select Wallpaper: Choose from 39 bundled studio backgrounds (Dark Studio,
     Cosmic, Gradient, Mesh, Sequoia, Sonoma, Ventura, abstracts, or transparent).
4. Camera & Zoom Engine:
   - Zoom Scale: 1.0x to 3.0x (Default: 1.8x)
   - Dwell Time Threshold: 400ms to 2000ms (Default: 800ms)
   - Auto-Pan Easing: Cubic Bézier / Spring
5. Cursor & VFX:
   - Cursor Pack: macOS, Windows 11, Tahoe, or Custom
   - Cursor Size: 50% to 200% (Default: 100%)
   - Motion Blur: Toggle Shutter Angle (180 deg)
   - Click Ripple Animation: Toggle expanding rings on mouse clicks
6. Click "Generate Motion Comp".
   - TraceMotion creates a dedicated project folder, imports assets, builds the
     multi-layer camera rig, calculates spring physics, sets hold keyframes for
     cursor states, places calibrated click ripples, and prepares the composition.

6. AFTER EFFECTS RIG ARCHITECTURE & CUSTOMIZATION
================================================================================
TraceMotion generates an industry-standard non-destructive layer hierarchy:

Layer Hierarchy:
----------------
1. TraceMotion_Camera_Controller (Null)
   - Contains live Expression Sliders for instant post-import tuning:
     * "Zoom Scale" (controls global zoom depth)
     * "Smoothness" (adjusts pan speed & spring easing)
     * "Corner Radius" (live window corner roundness)
     * "Shadow Opacity" (live shadow darkness)
     * "Cursor Scale" (live cursor size multiplier)
2. TraceMotion_Camera_Rig (3D Master Null)
   - Drives all synchronous pan and zoom motion for the window, shadows, video,
     cursor master, and ripples.
3. TraceMotion_Click_Ripples (Shape Layer parented to Rig)
   - Generates expanding color rings at exact click hotspots with ease-out opacity.
4. Cursor State Layers (parented to TraceMotion_Cursor_Master)
   - "Cursor_arrow", "Cursor_pointer", "Cursor_text", "Cursor_crosshair",
     "Cursor_resize-ew", "Cursor_resize-ns", "Cursor_open-hand",
     "Cursor_closed-hand", "Cursor_not-allowed", "Cursor_wait"
   - Each state layer has its anchor point calibrated to its exact pixel hotspot.
   - State switching is achieved via clean Hold Keyframes on layer Opacity.
5. TraceMotion_Cursor_Master (Null parented to Rig)
   - Carries spring-interpolated position coordinates.
   - Evaluates dynamic velocity-based sway rotation expressions.
   - Evaluates click depression bounce scale curves.
6. Clean_Screen_Capture (Video Footage parented to Rig)
   - The raw screen recording footage.
   - Track Matte: Set to Alpha Matte using "TraceMotion_Window_Matte".
7. TraceMotion_Window_Matte (Shape Layer parented to Rig)
   - Rounded rectangle vector mask matching exact video dimensions.
   - Roundness linked dynamically to the Controller's "Corner Radius" slider.
   - Fixed anchor point and coordinate origin prevents 1/4 screen clipping bugs.
8. TraceMotion_Window_Shadow (Shape Layer parented to Rig)
   - Independent 3-tier drop shadow system (Contact, Body, Ambient) placed behind
     the video. Shadows remain completely unclipped by the video's track matte.
9. TraceMotion_Background (Image/Video Layer)
   - Automatically scaled to fill the entire canvas with "cover" aspect-ratio fit.

7. BUILDING THE OBS PLUGIN FROM SOURCE (DEVELOPERS)
================================================================================
To modify or rebuild `tracemotion-obs.dll`:
1. Ensure Visual Studio 2022 (or VS 2026/Build Tools) with "Desktop development with C++"
   and the Windows 10/11 SDK is installed.
2. Open a standard Command Prompt (cmd.exe) or PowerShell.
3. Navigate to:
   cd "D:\all my code\TraceMotion\obs-telemetry-recorder"
4. Run:
   build.bat
5. The automated script will:
   - Initialize the MSVC x64 compiler environment.
   - Verify OBS import definitions (`obs.lib`, `obs-frontend-api.lib`).
   - Compile `src\main.cpp` and `src\telemetry_worker.cpp` with C++20 Release optimizations.
   - Link `user32.lib`, `gdi32.lib`, and `winmm.lib`.
   - Output `build\tracemotion-obs.dll`.

8. TROUBLESHOOTING & FAQ
================================================================================
Q: The After Effects panel gives an error: "Unable to execute script at line 1.
   Access to files is not allowed."
A: Go to AE "Edit" -> "Preferences" -> "Scripting & Expressions" and check
   "Allow Scripts to Write Files and Access Network".

Q: The screen capture looks zoomed into the top-left quadrant (1/4 screen).
A: This bug occurred in legacy importer versions due to mismatched shape anchor
   coordinates. Ensure you are using the updated `TraceMotionImporter.jsx` which
   sets `matteLayer.anchorPoint = [0, 0]` and `matteLayer.position = [w/2, h/2]`.

Q: Cursor states in the recording do not change (always shows Arrow).
A: Verify that you installed the newly built `tracemotion-obs.dll` into
   `C:\Program Files\obs-studio\obs-plugins\64bit\`. The hybrid geometric classifier
   in `telemetry_worker.cpp` detects custom browser cursors, text I-beams, hand pointers,
   and resize handles dynamically at 60Hz/120Hz across all Windows DPI scaling levels.

Q: Click ripples appear off-target.
A: The updated importer calculates click coordinates using normalized viewport ratios
   multiplied by the composition footage dimensions, anchored directly inside the
   shape vector transform group. Ensure your OBS source matches your screen resolution.

Q: Can I use custom wallpapers or cursor packs?
A: Yes!
   - Wallpapers: Place `.png`, `.jpg`, or `.mp4` files into `assets\wallpapers\` and
     add them to `manifest.json`.
   - Cursors: Add custom PNG cursor states into `assets\cursors\<pack_name>\` and
     define their pixel hotspots in `assets\cursors\manifest.json`.
